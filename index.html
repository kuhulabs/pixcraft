Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kuhu Labs ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use PixCraft - Photo Editor & Art ("the App").

By using PixCraft, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Photos & Media
PixCraft requires access to your device's photo gallery to allow you to edit, create collages, and save photos. We do not upload, store, or share your photos on any external server. All photo editing is done locally on your device.

b) Camera
The App requests camera permission to allow you to take photos directly within the app. Camera data is not stored or transmitted to any server.

c) Device Information
We may collect basic device information such as device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and crash reporting purposes.

d) Usage Data
We collect anonymous usage data to improve the app experience. This includes which features are used, session duration, and crash reports.

2. Third-Party Services

PixCraft uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

Google AdMob
We use Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show personalized ads. You can opt out via your device settings.
Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Firebase
We use Firebase for crash reporting and analytics. Firebase collects anonymous usage statistics.
Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

OneSignal
We use OneSignal to send push notifications. OneSignal may collect device tokens and notification interaction data.
Privacy Policy: https://onesignal.com/privacy_policy

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve the App's features
  • Show relevant advertisements
  • Send push notifications (only if you allow)
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience

4. Data Storage & Security

Your photos and edited content are stored only on your device. We do not upload your personal photos to any server. We implement appropriate security measures to protect any data we do collect.

5. Children's Privacy

PixCraft is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

6. Permissions We Request

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — To access photos for editing
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — To save edited photos to your gallery
  • CAMERA — To take photos within the app
  • INTERNET — To load advertisements and send notifications
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — To send push notifications (optional)

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke any permissions from your device settings at any time
  • Opt out of personalized ads via Google Ad Settings
  • Disable push notifications from device settings
  • Request deletion of any data by contacting us

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
